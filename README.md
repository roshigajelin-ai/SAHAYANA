#  Sahayana
### Accessibility. Safety. Dignity.

**A more inclusive world, one barrier at a time.**

## 🌍 About the Project

In India, everyday activities such as travelling on crowded public transport, accessing government services, communicating with others, and seeking help in unfamiliar or threatening situations can be challenging. For persons with disabilities, these challenges can create additional barriers to independence, safety, and dignity.

**Sahayana** is an inclusive accessibility and safety platform designed to help address these challenges through technology. It aims to bring essential accessibility tools, communication assistance, safety support, and public-service information together in one unified platform.

Our goal is to make everyday life more accessible, safer, and more inclusive for everyone.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Persons with disabilities often encounter barriers in public transportation, government procedures, communication, personal safety, and social inclusion. The lack of accessible information and convenient support systems can make everyday tasks difficult and limit independent participation in society.

Sahayana aims to bridge these gaps through a user-centered, inclusive technology solution.

## ✨ Key Features

- ♿ **Accessibility Support** — Make digital information and everyday services easier to access.
- 🚌 **Accessible Transportation** — Support users in finding relevant transport information and navigating travel challenges.
- 💬 **Inclusive Communication** — Help reduce communication barriers between users and service providers.
- 🏛️ **Government Service Assistance** — Make essential public-service information and procedures easier to understand.
- 🛡️ **Personal Safety Support** — Help users access emergency contacts and assistance when needed.
- 🤝 **Independence and Inclusion** — Encourage autonomy, equal opportunities, and respectful interactions.

*Note: These are the intended feature areas. Actual capabilities depend on the features implemented by the project team.*

## 🎯 Our Mission

To reduce everyday accessibility barriers and empower persons with disabilities to navigate their surroundings, communicate their needs, access essential services, and seek assistance with greater confidence and independence.

## 🔭 Our Vision

To create a society where accessibility is not a privilege, safety is not a concern, communication is not a barrier, and every individual is treated with dignity and respect.

## 💡 What Makes Sahayana Meaningful?

Sahayana focuses on more than accessibility alone. It brings together four essential principles:

- **Accessibility:** Access to information and essential services.
- **Safety:** Greater access to support when users need help.
- **Communication:** Opportunities to express needs and be understood.
- **Dignity:** Promoting independence, equality, and inclusion.
